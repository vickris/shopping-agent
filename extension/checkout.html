🛒 Shopping Agent Browser checkout

Your basket, in your browser

Sign in and pay directly on the supermarket website. The extension never reads your password, cookies or payment details.

    Only the selected products are set to the quantities listed here. Other existing basket items are kept. Prices and delivery may differ in your own store session; the transfer stops if a selected price changes. You review the full order and submit payment yourself. If the transfer stops, some items may already have been added; inspect the basket before continuing.

    Keep the helper supermarket tab open during transfer. Stopping an active transfer closes that helper tab but does not delete its cart. Basket instructions expire after 30 minutes.

    Select products in Shopping Agent, then choose “Checkout in my browser”.
