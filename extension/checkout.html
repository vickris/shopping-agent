🛒 Shopping Agent Browser checkout

Your basket, in your browser

Sign in and pay directly on the supermarket website. The extension never reads your password, cookies or payment details.

    Each new order replaces the entire supermarket cart, including items you added directly on its website. Sign in and confirm the delivery address first, then choose “Replace cart & build this order”. The extension checks selected prices, empties the old cart, adds this order and verifies its contents. If a price, stock limit or store control prevents this, it stops and shows the reason. You review the final total and pay yourself.

    Keep the helper supermarket tab open during transfer. Stopping an active transfer closes that helper tab but does not delete its cart. Basket instructions expire after 30 minutes. Sending a new order stops the previous transfer.

    Select products in Shopping Agent, then choose “Checkout in my browser”.
